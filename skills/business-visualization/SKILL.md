# Business Visualization

First classify as quantitative chart, strategic framework, or dashboard. Record metric definition, unit, denominator, time range, aggregation, data source, refresh cadence, and decision audience.

Never alter values or use misleading axes. Label actuals, forecasts, estimates, and illustrative assumptions distinctly. For TAM/SAM/SOM, show assumptions and calculation basis. Do not rank SWOT items or strategic factors without a defined method.

Vega-Lite suits declarative charts, ECharts interactive dashboards, D3 custom charts, Graphviz relationship-heavy models, and native SVG strategic frameworks such as SWOT and BCG. These external tools are optional.

Deliver SVG, source data/specification, assumptions, as-of date, and QA report.
